Privacy Policy

At Kuzi Box, accessible from our live domain, user privacy is one of our core priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of technical data collected and recorded by our platform and how we utilize it.

1. Log Files and Analytical Data

Kuzi Box follows a standard procedure of utilizing server log files. These files automatically log visitors when they navigate web architectures. The data collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser variants, Internet Service Provider (ISP) tags, date/time references, and referring/exit pages. This information is used strictly to analyze user traffic trends, administer the website layout, and optimize dynamic script processing.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any standard premium web application, Kuzi Box uses cookies to store parameters including visitor preferences and the specific sub-pages that the user accessed. This metadata allows us to enhance user experiences by customizing our dynamic interface content based on browser technicalities.

3. Third-Party Network Integration

Our platform indexes and embeds streaming players governed by third-party hosting networks. These external structures may utilize tracking cookies, script frameworks, or specialized scripts to measure the data delivery speed or to personalizing external configurations. Kuzi Box has no access to or structural control over the cookies utilized by these third-party video networks.

4. Data Protection Standards

We do not collect personal registration profiles, financial credentials, or identity documents. Your interactive operations within our platform remain completely anonymous and unmonitored by our static application stack.

5. Consent

By interacting with and utilizing our web directory framework, you hereby signify your comprehensive consent to our Privacy Policy and unconditionally agree to its operational terms.
